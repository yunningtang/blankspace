留白Blankspace

可用空间，是纸。Free space is the paper.

整个窗口是一张宣纸上的水墨：墨越深，占的空间越大；空着的纸，就是你的可用空间。清理之前，留白先告诉你每一项是什么、为什么、清了会怎样；清掉的东西只进回收站。 The window is a sheet of rice paper with ink on it: the darker the ink, the more space it takes, and the blank paper is your free space. Before anything is cleaned, Blankspace tells you what each item is, why, and what clearing it costs. Nothing goes further than the Recycle Bin.

它能做什么What it does

你选了地方，才去看It looks only where you choose

首页列出所有磁盘（C、D、移动硬盘）和常用文件夹。选一个，它在后台低优先级慢慢读，不拖慢电脑；可以暂停、取消。看过的地方记住结果，下次直接显示，不重复读硬盘。 The home page lists every drive (C, D, USB drives) and your common folders. Pick one and it reads slowly in the background at low priority, so your PC stays fast; pause or cancel any time. Places it has seen are remembered, so the disk isn’t read again.

最上面还有“一键清理”：只清临时文件、错误报告、缩略图这类可以放心清的。At the top there’s also One-click clean, for the truly safe things only: temporary files, error reports, thumbnails.

点一块墨，走进去Click the ink to walk in

每块墨是一个文件夹或文件，越大越深。点一下就进到那个文件夹，路径每一级都能点回去。右键可以打开、在文件夹中显示、复制路径、移到回收站、挪到别的盘。 Each patch of ink is a folder or file; bigger means darker. Click to step into a folder and click any part of the path to step back. Right-click to open, show in folder, copy the path, move to the Recycle Bin or move to another drive.

资源管理器里也能右键任何文件夹：“用留白看看这里”。In File Explorer, right-click any folder: “Look here with Blankspace”.

每个建议，都写“为什么”Every suggestion says why

“已经解压过了，旁边有同名的文件夹。”“安装文件，2025 年 10 月下载。”“和图片里那份一模一样。”说人话，不说路径和扩展名。你自己的文件从不默认选中。 “Already extracted: there’s a folder with the same name next to it.” “Installer, downloaded in Oct 2025.” “Identical to the copy in Pictures.” Plain words, not paths. Your own files are never selected for you.

动手之前，先问你It asks before it acts

按钮会原地变成一段说明：要动什么、多大、哪些不会动以及为什么。点确定才开始。默认是“只看”模式，要你在设置里打开，留白才会动文件。 The button turns into a short note right where it is: what will be touched, how much, and what won’t be and why. Nothing starts until you confirm. It ships in look-only mode; it touches files only after you allow it in Settings.

移到回收站不会马上腾出空间——清空回收站之后才会。留白不说“已释放”。Moving to the Recycle Bin doesn’t free space yet; emptying it does. Blankspace never claims “freed”.

缓存，写明代价Caches, with the cost spelled out

临时文件这类几乎没有代价的，默认选上。浏览器、开发工具、聊天软件的缓存默认不选，每一项写清楚清了会怎样：网页第一次打开会慢、依赖要重新下载。对应的程序开着，那一项就不清。 Things with almost no downside, like temporary files, are on by default. Browser, developer-tool and chat caches are off by default, and each says what clearing it costs: slower first page loads, dependencies downloaded again. If the program is running, its cache is skipped.

整理，而不只是删除Tidy, not just delete

IMG_2034、微信图片_… 改成拍摄时间；“终版”“最终版”“(1)”收进“旧版本”；截图录屏按月份收好；自己拍的视频挪到 D 盘。都只是挪位置、改名字，一键撤销。 IMG_2034 becomes the date it was taken; “final”, “final2” and “(1)” go into “Old versions”; screenshots are sorted by month; your own videos move to drive D. Only moves and renames, all undoable in one click.

它不会做什么What it won’t do

  • 不永久删除Delete anything for good 一律移到回收站。放不进回收站的（U 盘、网络盘、太大的文件）直接不动。Everything goes to the Recycle Bin. Anything that can’t (USB sticks, network drives, huge files) is left alone.
  • 不碰系统和程序Touch Windows or your programs Windows、Program Files、程序数据、OneDrive 一律不动。Windows, Program Files, program data and OneDrive are never touched.
  • 不替你决定Decide for you 你的文件从不默认选中；每一次都先列出要动什么。Your files are never preselected, and every action is listed first.
  • 不清空回收站Empty your Recycle Bin 清空了就找不回来，这一步留给你。Once emptied, things are gone, so that step stays yours.
  • 不在后台偷偷读硬盘Read your disk behind your back 打开时不扫描；你选了地方才看，而且只读。Nothing is scanned at launch; it reads only where you point it, read-only.
  • 不联网Phone home 没有账号、没有广告、不收集任何信息。代码全部公开。No account, no ads, nothing collected. All of the code is public.
